Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 August 2026

1. Introduction

Lime is a Discord bot and website designed to provide server management, moderation, protection, and logging functionality.

This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when Lime is used.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features enabled by a Discord server, Lime may process:

  • Discord user IDs
  • Discord usernames and display names
  • Discord server IDs
  • Role and permission information
  • Moderation actions
  • Configuration settings
  • Bot command usage
  • Security and audit logs

3. Why Information Is Used

Information may be processed to provide moderation, security, logging, configuration, and server-management features.

4. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the relevant Lime feature, maintain security records, investigate incidents, or comply with applicable obligations.

5. Data Sharing

Lime does not sell user information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate the bot, website, hosting infrastructure, or other services.

6. Discord

Lime operates through Discord's platform. Discord's own policies and terms may also apply when you interact with Lime through Discord.

7. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information processed by Lime. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

8. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Lime's services, features, or legal requirements change.
